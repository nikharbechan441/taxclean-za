1. Installation & Windows Setup

TaxClean ZA is distributed officially through the Microsoft Store (Store ID: 9NQKZNK24JD6) as a sandboxed Windows 10/11 MSIX application.

  1. Open the Microsoft Store on Windows 10 or Windows 11 and search for TaxClean - ZA (or click the official Store link on taxcleanza.com).
  2. Click Get / Install. Windows automatically verifies the Microsoft Store digital signature and installs the application into an isolated container with automatic updates.
  3. Click Open (or launch TaxClean - ZA from your Start Menu). Press F1 or click [ 📖 Manual / How to Use ] above the transaction table at any time to open the built-in interactive guide.

2. Free Google AI Studio Key Setup (1-Click Sentinel)

TaxClean ZA operates 100% locally and offline by default. For rare, difficult, or handwritten scanned documents, it includes an optional Google Gemini Flash Micro-AI Resolver where you can connect your own free Google AI Studio key.

  1. In the top-left corner of the app, click the [ 🔑 AI Auto-Key ] button.
  2. Click "Get Free Key (Opens Google AI Studio)".
  3. Sign in with your Google account, click Create API Key, and copy the key to your clipboard.
  4. Automatic Sentinel: TaxClean's background sentinel temporarily monitors your clipboard for up to 90 seconds to detect the copied AIza... key, verifies it, encrypts it via Windows DPAPI, and closes the wizard with zero manual typing!

3. Simultaneous Multi-Document Import (Cent-Zero Reconciliation)

TaxClean supports all South African retail and commercial banks AND lets you select and import multiple documents at the exact same time:

  • First National Bank (FNB): Cheque / Current, Credit Card, and Easy Accounts.
  • Capitec Bank: Savings & Mercantile Business Accounts.
  • Standard Bank: Current, Business & Credit.
  • Nedbank & Absa: Enterprise & Commercial formats.
  • Discovery Bank, Investec & TymeBank: Modern digital statement layouts + scanned till slips & PDF invoices.

How to Import One or Multiple Documents Simultaneously:

  1. Click [ ⚡ Import Docs ] in the top navigation bar (or press Ctrl+O).
  2. In the file picker, hold Ctrl or Shift (or drag a selection box) to select as many PDF bank statements, CSVs, and scanned till slips/invoices as you want at once, then click Open.
  3. TaxClean processes every selected file in a single batch, automatically sorts all transactions chronologically, removes overlapping duplicate rows across files via the Duplicate Shield, and links opening/closing balances across consecutive statements.
  4. Check the Audit Reconciliation Bar:
    Mathematical Proof of Balance:
    Opening Balance + Total Receipts - Total Payments = Closing Balance.
    When the variance badge shows Δ R 0.00 in green, your batch is mathematically reconciled to the cent!

4. Chart of Accounts Manager (Add, Edit & Delete GL Accounts)

Customize your General Ledger codes, account names, default SARS VAT rules, and auto-matching keywords to match your exact Sage Pastel, Xero, or custom practice Chart of Accounts.

  1. Click the teal [ 📋 Chart of Accounts ] button in the filter bar directly above the transaction table (or press Alt+C, or open 🛠️ Tools ▾ → 📋 Chart of Accounts Manager).
  2. ➕ Add a New GL Account: Enter the GL Code (e.g., 4650/000), Account Name (e.g., Courier & Freight), Default VAT Rule (Standard 15%, Zero-Rated 0%, Exempt, or Out of Scope), Ledger Type, and optional comma-separated Auto-Match Keywords (e.g., THE COURIER GUY, ARAMEX, DHL), then click [ ➕ Add / Save GL Account ].
  3. ✏️ Edit an Existing Account: Click any account row in the Chart of Accounts table to load its details into the editor, modify any field, and click [ ➕ Add / Save GL Account ] to update it immediately across the entire app.
  4. 🗑️ Delete GL Accounts: Select one or more accounts in the list (hold Ctrl or Shift for multi-select) and click [ 🗑️ Delete Selected Account(s) ] (or press the Delete key). Note: The system Suspense Account (9999/000) is protected so unallocated rows always have a safe fallback.
  5. 📂 Import CSV or Reset: Use [ 📂 Import COA from CSV ] to bulk-import a client's Chart of Accounts from Excel/Pastel/Xero, or click [ 🔄 Reset to SA Default COA ] at any time.

5. Multi-Month Stacking for SARS Bi-Monthly VAT201

South African VAT vendors file returns bi-monthly (every 2 months). You can either select all months at once via [ ⚡ Import Docs ], or stack additional statements onto an existing open session at any time:

  1. Click [ ➕ Append ] (or press Alt+A) in the top navigation bar.
  2. Select one or multiple additional monthly statements (hold Ctrl or Shift to pick multiple files).
  3. The Lockstep Continuity Check: TaxClean verifies that Month 1 Closing Balance matches Month 2 Opening Balance to the exact cent.
  4. If there is an overlap, the Duplicate Shield automatically filters out duplicated transactions, giving you a seamless, unified multi-month ledger ready for eFiling.

6. "Shoebox" Receipt-to-Bank Auto-Matcher

No more manually hunting through piles of paper slips to match bank debit transactions.

  1. Click [ 📎 Match Receipts ] in the navbar (or press Alt+M).
  2. Select the folder or files containing your client's scanned slips, till receipts, and PDF tax invoices (supports JPG, PNG, WEBP, and PDF).
  3. The built-in local OCR and Receipt Matcher reads invoice dates, supplier VAT numbers, and gross totals.
  4. TaxClean automatically pairs each slip with its matching bank payment row and attaches a 📎 paperclip badge.
  5. Audit Verification: Right-click any row in the ledger and select "View Attached Receipt / Slip" to open and inspect the original source document immediately.
  6. Audit Risk Alert: The summary tab highlights any outflow > R 50 that lacks a supporting receipt, helping your practice review documentary requirements under SARS Section 16(2).

7. SARS VAT201 eFiling Box Summary Card

Generate reconciled SARS VAT201 working paper figures in 1 click:

  1. Click [ 🏛️ VAT201 ] in the navbar (or press Alt+V).
  2. The modal displays the reconciled SARS eFiling working paper figures:
    • Box 1: Standard Rated Supplies (Inclusive of 15% VAT).
    • Box 4 / 13: Output Tax on Standard Rated Supplies.
    • Box 2: Zero-Rated Supplies (Fuel Levy, Exports, Basic Foods).
    • Box 3: Exempt Supplies (Interest & Pure Financial Services; excludes standard 15% bank service fees).
    • Box 15: Claimable Standard Rated Input Tax on Other Goods & Services.
    • Box 19: Total Input Tax Deductions.
    • Box 20: Net VAT Payable or Refund Due.
  3. Click the [ 📋 Copy ] button next to any box to copy the number directly to your clipboard for pasting into SARS eFiling after practitioner review.

8. Multi-Account Transaction Splits

Split a single bank outflow across multiple general ledger accounts (e.g., Makro purchases split between office consumables and owner drawings).

  1. Select the transaction in the ledger grid.
  2. Press Alt+S (or click Tools > Split Selected Transaction).
  3. Click [ ➕ Add Split Line ] to subdivide the amount.
  4. Or select a 1-Click Preset:
    • 50 / 50 Split
    • 60 / 40 Split
    • Payroll Settlement Split: Separates bulk salary withdrawals into Net Wages, PAYE Withholding, and statutory-capped UIF Liability.
  5. The mathematical validation lock guarantees total child lines equal parent amount to R 0.00 before saving.

9. Exporting to Accounting Software (Pastel, Xero, ERPs)

Click [ 💾 Export ] (or press Ctrl+E) to launch the Pre-Flight Export Validator.

Accounting System Format Specifications Import Instructions
Sage Pastel Partner Official 10-column ASCII Cashbook layout. Strict no-header rule, standard 7-digit account codes (1000000), Pastel tax indices (1 = 15%, 2 = 0%, 0 = Exempt). Open Pastel > Process > Cash Books > Select Bank > Batch > Import.
Sage Business Cloud (Sage One) Official 3-column CSV: Date (DD/MM/YYYY), Description, Amount (negative = payments, positive = receipts). Open Sage One > Banking > Upload a Statement > Select CSV file.
Xero Official 6-column bank feed CSV: Date, Amount, Payee, Description, Reference, Check Number. Open Xero > Accounting > Bank Accounts > Manage Account > Import a Statement.
QuickBooks Online Official 3-column CSV: Date, Description, Amount. Open QuickBooks > Banking > Link Account > Upload from file.
SARS Excel Workpaper Multi-tab formatted Excel workbook containing VAT201 return schedule and detailed cashbook audit trail. Directly printable / attachable for SARS tax audits and annual financial statements.

10. SARS Section 29 Audit Batch Locking

When bookkeeping write-ups are finalized, you can freeze the batch to prevent accidental modifications:

  1. Click Tools > 1-Click Lock Batch & Integrity Report (or press Alt+L).
  2. TaxClean calculates a cryptographic SHA-256 digital fingerprint across all transactions and balances.
  3. The session is locked against further edits.
  4. Generates a printable SARS TAA Section 29 Electronic Record Integrity Report documenting reviewer details, bank reconciliation proof, and SHA-256 hash verification.
