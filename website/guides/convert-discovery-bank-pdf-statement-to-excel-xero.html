Why Discovery Bank Statements are Difficult to Reconcile

Discovery Bank is popular among professionals and high-growth businesses due to its dynamic interest rates and Vitality Money rewards integration. However, monthly PDF statements generated by the Discovery Banking app present severe formatting challenges for bookkeepers:

  • Embedded Vitality Money Points: Statements interleave actual ZAR cash transactions with Vitality Miles earning summaries, spend boost cashbacks, and partner discounts. Generic PDF converters import these as regular bank transactions, distorting closing balances.
  • Multi-Line FX Transactions: Foreign card payments (e.g. Google, Zoom, Microsoft) are rendered across 3 distinct sub-rows displaying original foreign currency amount, exchange rate markup, and final settled Rand amount.
  • Dynamic Daily Interest Lines: Discovery calculates and displays daily interest rate changes within the statement grid.

Converting to Xero and Sage Pastel

To successfully import Discovery statements without manual editing:

  1. Drop the PDF into TaxClean with the "Discovery Bank" profile selected.
  2. TaxClean automatically filters out Vitality Miles rewards and interest rate notices.
  3. Multi-line foreign transactions are collapsed into a single row with the final ZAR settlement amount and the foreign currency referenced in the description.
  4. Download the balanced 5-column Xero CSV or formatted Sage Pastel Cashbook batch import file.
