Privacy Policy & Data Architecture

Last Updated: November 2026  |  Operator: TaxClean Technologies South Africa (https://taxcleanza.com)

This Privacy Policy governs both the TaxClean Web Application (https://taxcleanza.com and its regional pages) and the TaxClean Desktop Application (Windows & macOS). We believe in complete technical transparency regarding what stays inside your device memory and what network requests occur when you use optional features.

1. Three-Tier Bank Statement Processing Architecture

  • Tier 1 — Default Local Browser & Desktop RAM Parsing (0 Bytes of PDF Uploaded): When you select or drag-and-drop a digital PDF bank statement into TaxClean, text extraction (via client-side pdf.js), running-balance reconciliation, tax categorization, and Excel/CSV/ERP file generation (via client-side SheetJS) execute 100% locally inside your web browser's volatile memory (RAM) or desktop workstation. When the local parser recognizes your statement layout, zero bytes of your PDF, account numbers, or transaction descriptions are uploaded to any server.
  • Tier 2 — Local In-Browser WebAssembly OCR (0 Bytes of PDF Uploaded): If a PDF contains scanned images or outlined vector fonts with no embedded text layer, TaxClean first executes client-side optical character recognition (Tesseract.js WebAssembly on Web, or Windows Local OCR on Desktop) directly inside your device memory without transmitting your statement to our servers.
  • Tier 3 — Strictly Opt-In Cloud AI OCR Fallback (Google Gemini API): If a complex scan, camera photo, or unstructured statement cannot be parsed locally in Tier 1 or Tier 2, TaxClean displays an explicit Opt-In Consent Prompt before any statement data leaves your device. Only if you explicitly click to approve Cloud AI OCR (or initiate a live AI Vision camera scan) will the extracted text (up to 45,000 characters) or up to 2 compressed page images and filename be transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.3) to our Cloudflare Worker endpoint (/api/ai-extract) and forwarded to the Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com, operated by Google LLC in the United States/global edge) solely to return structured transaction rows. TaxClean does not log, store, or retain statement contents in Cloudflare KV or any database. Processing by Google LLC is subject to the Google Gemini API Terms. Under POPIA Section 72 and EU/UK GDPR Chapter V (Art. 49(1)(a)), this cross-border transfer occurs strictly upon your explicit per-file consent.

2. Billing, Subscriptions & Payment Processors (Yoco & PayFast)

When you purchase a 7-Day Pass, Pro Unlimited monthly plan, Practice & Firm plan, or Desktop annual license:

  • Payment Gateway: Web checkouts are processed via Yoco Technologies (Pty) Ltd (payments.yoco.com, settled in South African Rand, ZAR, at your card issuer's exchange rate). Desktop licenses may also be settled via EFT or PayFast (Pty) Ltd. TaxClean never collects, views, or stores your payment card numbers, CVV, or banking PINs.
  • Subscription Records Stored in Cloudflare KV: To verify your paid access across sessions, we store a minimal subscription record in encrypted Cloudflare Workers KV (sub_<email> and chk_<orderRef>) containing your Work Email Address, Company Name (if provided), Plan SKU, Order Reference, Checkout ID, Amount Paid, and Expiry Timestamp. In Cloudflare KV, chk_<orderRef> checkout verification records expire automatically after 30 days, 7-Day Pass sub_<email> records expire after 7 days, and monthly subscription sub_<email> records expire after 35 days per billing cycle. Official commercial Tax Invoices are generated client-side in your browser so you can download and retain them for the 5-year statutory period required under Section 29 of the South African Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011.

TaxClean stores your preferences locally inside your own browser's localStorage (including tcza_custom_categories, tcza_vendor_rules, tcza_free_used, tcza_region_override, tcza_lang_override, and your signed tcza_pro_token / tcza_order_ref). You can wipe all saved browser data with one click using the "Clear Saved Browser Data" button in the Privacy & Terms modal.
